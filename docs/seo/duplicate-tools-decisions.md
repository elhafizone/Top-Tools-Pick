# Merge decisions by icon rule

Rule (owner): a product with its own distinct icon keeps its own page (URL = product name only) and links to the company; a product with no icon of its own goes into the company page.

**How icon was judged:** logo images were downloaded and compared visually. Same image as a sibling = no icon of its own. Most pages use a domain favicon, which is identical for every product of one domain, so those count as sharing.
